How to use SkyBook

Three roles, three flows — all on-chain.

1

Admin

Approves airline addresses with addAirline() to allow them to list flights.

2

Airline

Lists flights via addFlight() with route, departure, price, and seat count.

3

Passenger

Calls book(flightId) sending exactly the flight price in BOT to receive a ticket.

4

Travel

Airline calls checkIn(ticketId) at boarding. Revenue is claimable any time.
